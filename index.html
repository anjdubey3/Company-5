    Our Story

    Granmark Certification Pvt. Ltd. is an independent certification body established as per the requirements of ISO/IEC 17021-1 (Conformity Assessment-Requirements for Bodies providing Audit and Certification of Management Systems). Granmark Certification is a fast-growing management certification body in India. We offer a range of certification schemes that are focused on providing the manufacturer and supplier with a pathway to meet market requirements, whether they are based on regulation or customer-driven. Granmark Certification is a company registered under the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013. This means that your certification process is less time-consuming, less costly, and more rewarding for your organisation and staff. It also positively supports your organisations ability to provide quality care and support for clients. Granmark serves internationally in the field of management systems for ISO certification services. Granmark has a vision of quality transformation through its services, and right now we are providing management systems for ISO certification services worldwide.

    Our quality policy statement for Management System for ISO Certification

    We are an independent Certification Body, which aims to provide an impartial, realistic and practical approach to its client’s certification requirements. GRANMARK will operate a management system in compliance with the requirements of ISO 17021-1 & IAF, Carryout certification services in a non-discriminatory manner, provide auditing services as well as Certification as per the accreditation scope and Compliance Certification and within the requirements of ISO 17021-1 :2015 & ISO 19011:2018 which is independent and impartial with high integrity. GRANMARK will undertake continual improvement in the way we conduct our business and the services we offer. GRANMARK shall avoid imposing undue financial or other conditions on clients or potential clients. GRANMARK shall train the staff to understand the philosophy of GRANMARK, and fulfill their talents, whilst operating to agree formalized management systems. Organization seeking certification services shall have access to GRANMARK services. In addition, access to all certification services shall not be restricted on the grounds that the applicant does not apply for other services or it is member of particular group or association.
